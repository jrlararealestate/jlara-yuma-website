Color Palette

Black, red, and room to breathe

Our color is deliberately simple — it looks premium and it's easy for the whole team to stay consistent. Red is the accent, not the whole page. White space is a feature.

Soft backgrounds. Pure #FFFFFF for logo space.

Color balance

  • ~70% black & white, ~20% neutral, ~10% red
  • Use red to point the eye: one button, one word, one line
  • Keep big areas clean — let the logo lead

Avoid

  • Red walls of color or red body text
  • Gradients, drop shadows, or neon on the logo
  • Random off-brand colors from a template

Protect the mark

Clear space & minimum size

ELEVATE

Keep a clear margin around the logo equal to the height of the letter “E”. Nothing crowds it — no text, no photos, no edges.

ELEVATE

Minimum sizes: full lockup no smaller than 140px / 1.2in wide. Below that, switch to the monogram so it stays crisp.

The Rules

Do & Don't

Do

  • Use the approved files straight from the brand folder
  • Give the logo clear space and a clean background
  • Pick the lockup that fits the space (stacked / horizontal / badge / monogram)
  • Keep it black, white, or on-red only
  • Post everything bilingual when it fits the audience

Don't

  • Stretch, squish, rotate, or recolor the logo
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows, or gradients
  • Put the black logo on a busy photo (use reversed or the badge)
  • Retype “ELEVATE” in a different font
  • Screenshot the logo — always use the real file
